Research/Training

Training

Decentralised learning from distributed, unlabelled data.

Many devices and organisations collect data that is difficult to label or pool. We study how to train models collaboratively while data stays local, including how to route model updates between peers and how differences in local data affect the robustness of self-supervised learning.

Selected publications

  • DeNAV: Decentralized Self-Supervised Learning with a Training Navigator

    Xuanyu Chen, Nan Yang, Charles Z. Liu, Dong Yuan

    Machine Learning · 2026

    DeNAV trains models across peers that keep their own unlabelled data. Its navigator selects the next client for each lightweight model, and its aggregation procedure accounts for how recently each model was updated. This lets several models train across the network without central coordination.

  • FedIL: Federated Incremental Learning from Decentralized Unlabeled Data with Convergence Analysis

    Nan Yang, Dong Yuan, Charles Z. Liu, Yongkun Deng, Wei Bao

    Preprint · 2023

    FedIL studies semi-supervised federated training when labels are available only at the server. Clients use agreement between the server and local models to select pseudo-labels, then build a local set of examples whose predicted labels stay consistent over time.

    Preprint

  • Hierarchical Federated Learning With Momentum Acceleration in Multi-Tier Networks

    Zhengjie Yang, Sen Fu, Wei Bao, Dong Yuan, Albert Y. Zomaya

    IEEE Transactions on Parallel and Distributed Systems · 2023

    HierMo adds momentum at the worker, edge and cloud tiers of a federated system. The study also examines how often to aggregate models at each tier when training time is limited.

    Paper

  • Distributed Online Multi-Label Learning with Privacy Protection in Internet of Things

    Fan Huang, Nan Yang, Huaming Chen, Wei Bao, Dong Yuan

    Applied Sciences · 2023

    This method trains multi-label classifiers across connected IoT devices as new observations arrive. Each device updates its model using local data and information from neighbours, keeping its raw observations on the device.

  • Threats and Defenses in the Federated Learning Life Cycle: A Comprehensive Survey and Challenges

    Yanli Li, Zhongliang Guo, Nan Yang, Huaming Chen, Dong Yuan, Weiping Ding

    IEEE Transactions on Neural Networks and Learning Systems · 2025

    This survey traces privacy and reliability risks across the federated learning life cycle, from participant selection and local training to aggregation and deployment. It compares defensive approaches, discusses the trade-offs that affect their practical use, and identifies open questions for reliable federated learning services.
